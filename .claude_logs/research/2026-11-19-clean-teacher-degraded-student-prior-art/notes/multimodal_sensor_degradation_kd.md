# Prior work: distillation / clean–corrupted consistency for robustness to DEGRADED sensors in multi-modal perception

Legend for the "training category" used below:
(a) clean-input teacher distilled into a corrupted/incomplete-input student; (b) consistency between a clean pass and a corrupted pass of the same network; (c) corruption augmentation / modality dropout only, no distillation.
"Verified" = read in the paper text (arXiv full text via alphaXiv) during this session. "Unverified" = from secondary citation, abstract only, or memory.

Reference scheme being checked ("the Scheme"): frozen clean-trained teacher sees clean RGB+depth+event+LiDAR (or RGB+LiDAR+event+radar); student sees a copy where each sensor is independently hit by a sensor-specific synthetic degradation (camera blur/exposure, depth holes, LiDAR beam drop, event down-sampling) or removed; KL on logits + supervised loss.

## Key Question 1 — Camera–LiDAR(-radar) fusion robustness for driving: which works distill from a clean model/pass, which only augment or drop?

### Takeaway
In camera–LiDAR 3D detection / BEV the dominant recipe is category (c): modality dropout ("masked-modal training", "switched modality training", L+C/L/C mixing) or stochastic corruption augmentation, combined with an architectural change (routers, experts, gates). Explicit clean→corrupted consistency for multi-sensor input does exist but outside 3D detection: VG-SAF (camera+LiDAR end-to-end driving, Aug 2026 preprint) pulls a noisy branch to a stop-gradient clean branch, and Robo3D does full-teacher/partial-student consistency for LiDAR only.

### Cited Findings
- **Grace-BEV — "Can BEV Perception Gracefully Degrade under Sensor Failures?"** (Haifa Zhang, Yijing Wang, Zhiqiang Zuo; Tianjin Univ.; arXiv 2605.30983v2, preprint). Task: camera–LiDAR 3D detection on LSS detectors (BEVFusion-MIT/AD, GraphBEV); eval nuScenes-R, nuScenes-C. Training = three-phase schedule with **modality dropout only** (0.5 both / 0.25 LiDAR emptied / 0.25 images zero-filled); no distillation, no corruption augmentation. Category (c). Verified. — [arXiv 2605.30983](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30983)
- Grace-BEV's related-work section states that for robustness "a common practice is MD [modality dropout]" and that it adopts "the MD distribution used in CMT: 0.25 camera-only, 0.25 LiDAR-only, and 0.50 multimodal"; it lists MetaBEV, RobBEV, UniBEV, MoME, MEFormer as architectural approaches, and describes knowledge distillation in this area only as cross-modal transfer (MonoDistill LiDAR→mono, CMDFusion image→LiDAR), i.e. not clean→corrupted. — [arXiv 2605.30983](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30983)
- **MoME — "Resilient Sensor Fusion under Adverse Sensor Failures via Multi-Modal Expert Fusion"** (Konyul Park, Yecheol Kim, Daehun Kim, Jun Won Choi; CVPR 2025; arXiv 2503.19776). Camera–LiDAR 3D detection, nuScenes / nuScenes-R / nuScenes-C. Stage 1 trains three expert decoders with no sensor-drop augmentation; stage 2 freezes them and trains the Adaptive Query Router with **synthetic sensor drop** (1/3 LiDAR drop, 1/3 camera drop, 1/3 both kept) and a cross-entropy routing label. No distillation, no clean/corrupted consistency. Category (c). Verified from alphaXiv report (venue from Grace-BEV reference list). — [arXiv 2503.19776](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19776)
- MoME's own summary of prior work: MetaBEV, RobBEV, UniBEV and CMT rely on "successive cross-modal attention, mutual deformable attention, channel-normalized feature alignment, and masked-modal training". — [arXiv 2503.19776](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19776)
- **MetaBEV — "Solving Sensor Failures for BEV Detection and Map Segmentation"** (Chongjian Ge, Junsong Chen, Enze Xie, Zhongdao Wang, Lanqing Hong, Huchuan Lu, Zhenguo Li, Ping Luo; ICCV 2023; arXiv 2304.09801). Camera–LiDAR, 3D detection + BEV map segmentation. Uses "switched-modality training" to simulate missing sensors; six corruptions (limited field, beam reduction, missing objects, view drop, view noise, obstacle occlusion) + two missing-sensor cases are evaluated zero-shot and, for an "in-domain test", the model is trained "on corrupted data with random degrees". No teacher, no consistency loss found in the text. Category (c). Verified (grep of full text; did not read every page). — [arXiv 2304.09801](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.09801)
- **SB-BEVFusion** (Markus Essl et al.; JKU Linz / MBZUAI; arXiv 2605.11799, short conference-format paper, venue unverified). Camera–LiDAR 3D detection, nuScenes + MultiCorrupt. Shared downstream BEV encoder trained on "a shuffled mixture of L+C/L/C"; upstream encoders frozen; corruptions (beam reduction, fog, motion blur, spatial/temporal misalignment) are evaluation-only. Category (c), modality-availability mixing only. Verified. — [arXiv 2605.11799](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11799)
- **PFS — "Post Fusion Bird's Eye View Feature Stabilization for Robust Multimodal 3D Detection"** (Trung Tien Dong, Dev Thakkar, Arman Sargolzaei, Xiaomin Lin; Univ. of South Florida; arXiv 2603.05623). Plug-in on frozen BEVFusion / UniBEV. Trained with **stochastic camera and LiDAR corruptions applied independently with probability 0.30** (rain lens, low light, occlusion, dropout; beam reduction, range dropout, sector dropout, miscalibration), staged curriculum, detection loss + an "Anchor Loss" that supervises a reliability map toward 1.0 on clean samples and toward the per-cell preserved-point ratio on corrupted ones. No clean teacher, no logit/feature distillation. Category (c) with reliability supervision. Verified from alphaXiv report only (not the PDF). — [arXiv 2603.05623](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05623)
- PFS's related-work summary: "CMT and UniTR employ masked-modality training and stochastic dropout"; MetaBEV/UniBEV are architectural redesigns; MoME is expert-based decoding. — [arXiv 2603.05623](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05623)
- **RoboFusion — "Towards Robust Multi-Modal 3D Object Detection via SAM"** (Ziying Song, Guoxing Zhang, Lin Liu, Lei Yang, Shaoqing Xu, Caiyan Jia, Feiyang Jia, Li Wang; IJCAI 2024; arXiv 2401.03907). Camera–LiDAR 3D detection, KITTI-C / nuScenes-C (27 corruptions, evaluation only: "[Dong et al.] has only added noise to the validation dataset and kept the train and test datasets clear"). Robustness comes from the SAM image encoder, "SAM-AD" pre-training (DMAE-style denoising masked auto-encoding where the input is a weather-corrupted image — rain/snow/fog/sunlight, severity 1–5 — and the target is the clean image), wavelet denoising and adaptive fusion. SAM is fine-tuned end-to-end (not frozen). No teacher–student distillation. Verified. — [arXiv 2401.03907](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.03907)
- **RoboDistill — "Towards robust multimodal 3D object detection via visual foundation models"** (Ziying Song, Lin Liu, Hongyu Pan, Shaoqing Xu, Lei Yang, Mingzhe Guo, Caiyan Jia; arXiv 2609.23541, Sep 2026; journal extension of RoboFusion). Adds "KD Fusion, in which the pretrained SAM-AD serves as a teacher that distills high-quality visual knowledge into a lightweight point-cloud network" (weight α=0.5, temperature τ=4). The teacher/student differ by **modality/network (VFM image branch → point-cloud network)**, not by clean vs corrupted input; detector training data stay clean. Not the Scheme. Verified. — [arXiv 2609.23541](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23541)
- **VG-SAF — "Variance-Guided Spatial Attention Fusion for Robust End-to-End Driving under Asymmetric Sensor Degradation"** (Weizhi Tao, Zengwang Jin, Xiao Wang, Hailong Huang; arXiv 2608.24366, preprint, Aug 2026). Camera + LiDAR end-to-end driving (CARLA Longest6) with dense perception heads. "A physically grounded augmentor injects representative camera and LiDAR faults and, alongside every augmented frame, emits a continuous corruption mask"; training uses a batch "[clean|noisy]", a "confidence-aware consistency term that pulls the noisy branch back to the clean prediction" with "the stop-gradient operator prevent[ing] the noisy branch from reshaping the clean reference", plus "cross-branch dense distillation" of the predicted variance (noisy variance matched to clean variance on clean pixels, inflated on destroyed pixels). Same network, stop-gradient clean pass (not a separate frozen teacher). **Category (b), multi-sensor, sensor-specific synthetic faults.** Verified by grep of full text (details of exact loss form and which outputs are matched not fully read). — [arXiv 2608.24366](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24366)
- **UP-Fuse — "Uncertainty-guided LiDAR-Camera Fusion for 3D Panoptic Segmentation"** (Rohit Mohan, Daniele Cattaneo, Florian Drews, Yakov Miron, Abhinav Valada; Univ. Freiburg / Bosch; arXiv 2602.19349). Pairs each original image with a corrupted counterpart (brightness/contrast shifts, sensor dropout, histogram matching to Dark Zurich); both go through the **frozen** camera encoder, and an MLP is trained (Huber loss) to predict the L2 feature divergence between the clean and corrupted features; this predicted "uncertainty" down-weights camera features in fusion. Clean/corrupted pairs are used to supervise an uncertainty estimator, **not** to distill predictions; only the camera is degraded. Verified from alphaXiv report. — [arXiv 2602.19349](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.19349)
- **Robo3D — "Towards Robust and Reliable 3D Perception against Corruptions"** (Lingdong Kong, Youquan Liu, Xin Li, Runnan Chen, Wenwei Zhang, Jiawei Ren, Liang Pan, Kai Chen, Ziwei Liu; ICCV 2023; arXiv 2303.17597). LiDAR-only benchmark (fog, wet ground, snow, motion blur, beam missing, crosstalk, incomplete echo, cross-sensor) for segmentation and detection. Proposes a "density-insensitive training framework": "The 'full' and 'partial' point clouds are fed into the teacher branch and student branch, respectively … the latter is generated by randomly masking the original point cloud", with completion and confirmation consistency losses between the two branches' predictions. Category (a)/(b) but **single-modality LiDAR**. Verified (grep; whether the teacher is EMA or frozen not read). — [arXiv 2303.17597](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17597)
- **Diff-KD** (Pengcheng Lyu, Chaokun Zhang, Gong Chen, Tao Tang, Zhaoxiang Luo; Tianjin Univ.; arXiv 2604.02061). Multi-agent collaborative LiDAR perception (OPV2V, DAIR-V2X) under seven Robo3D corruptions. Teacher = early-fusion holistic-view model, student = per-agent local views; KL on fused features and cls/reg outputs + conditional diffusion feature restoration. "Diff-KD is trained exclusively on clean data and evaluated on all corruption variants without any fine-tuning." So the teacher/student gap is holistic-vs-local view, **not clean-vs-corrupted**. Its related work describes DSRC (AAAI 2025, arXiv 2412.10739) as "a sparse-to-dense distillation framework to learn density-insensitive … representations against real-world corruptions". Verified. — [arXiv 2604.02061](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02061)
- Search-level leads not opened: SARFusion (arXiv 2609.29235, scene-aware routing, camera–LiDAR), CRUISE (arXiv 2608.09202, VLM-guided uncertainty fusion), "SAMDistill: SAM-based spatial-temporal distillation for robust 3D…" (2026 journal paper evaluated on nuScenes-C; student design not visible in snippet), MSC-Bench (arXiv 2501.01037, 16 corruption types for camera–LiDAR, benchmark). — [alphaXiv 2609.29235](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2609.29235); [alphaXiv 2608.09202](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2608.09202); [SAMDistill record](https://pure.nwpu.edu.cn/en/publications/samdistill-sam-based-spatial-temporal-distillation-for-robust-3d-/); [arXiv 2501.01037](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.01037)

### Inferences
- For camera–LiDAR 3D detection/BEV, none of the works whose text was read (Grace-BEV, MoME, MetaBEV, SB-BEVFusion, PFS, RoboFusion, RoboDistill) uses a clean teacher distilled into a corrupted-input fusion student; robustness is obtained by dropout/augmentation plus architecture. Two independent web searches for "clean teacher → corrupted multi-modal student" on nuScenes-C returned no such paper.
- The clean-pass/noisy-pass idea is nevertheless present in multi-sensor driving (VG-SAF, 2026) and in LiDAR-only perception (Robo3D, 2023), so "clean reference supervises degraded input" cannot be claimed as new for driving perception in general.
- Radar-inclusive fusion robustness with distillation: nothing found.

## Key Question 2 — Multi-modal semantic segmentation under adverse conditions / sensor failure (DELIVER, MUSES, MCubeS, MFNet, FMB, PST900): synthetic degradation in training? clean teacher?

### Takeaway
On exactly the Scheme's benchmarks, the "frozen full-modality teacher → student with perturbed input, KL on logits (+ features/prototypes)" structure already exists (AnySeg; RobustSeg/RMMSS; FuseAdapt's "Output KD" control; M3L), but in every verified case the student perturbation is **whole-modality dropout (or patch masking), not sensor-specific synthetic degradation**. Noisy/degraded modalities are, in this literature, an evaluation protocol (NM in the 2503.18445 benchmark; DELIVER's built-in corner cases), not a training-time student input.

### Cited Findings
- **AnySeg — "Learning Robust Anymodal Segmentor with Unimodal and Cross-modal Distillation"** (Xu Zheng, Haiwei Xue, Jialei Chen, Yibo Yan, Lutao Jiang, Yuanhuiyi Lyu, Kailun Yang, Linfeng Zhang, Xuming Hu; arXiv 2411.17141v2, "Preprint. Under review"). DELIVER (R/D/E/L) and MUSES (F/E/L), SegFormer-B0. Two stages: a multimodal teacher is pre-trained with parallel multimodal learning and "its parameter is frozen during training the student"; the teacher takes the full mini-batch {r,d,l,e}; the student input "undergoes random masking to generate an anymodal batch, where modality data is randomly dropped". Distilled: multi-scale unimodal features (KL after softmax), cross-modal cosine-similarity correspondences (KL), and a prediction-level "modality-agnostic distillation". **No synthetic degradation of the student input** — only dropout. DELIVER corner cases (motion blur, over/under-exposure, LiDAR jitter, event low-res) are evaluation conditions (Table 9). Category (a) with missing-only student. Verified. — [arXiv 2411.17141](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.17141)
- **RMMSS / RobustSeg** (Jiaqi Tan, Xu Zheng, Yang Liu; BUPT / HKUST(GZ) / INSAIT; arXiv 2505.12861). v1 title "Robust Multimodal Segmentation with Representation Regularization and Hybrid Prototype Distillation" (RobustSeg); v2 "RMMSS: … Hybrid Prototype Distillation and Feature Selection" (carries an AAAI 2026 copyright line). Stage 1: "we pre-train the teacher model with full-modality data", then a student with "Anymodal Dropout" is trained with L = L_CE + λ·L_KL (logit KL to the teacher) + α·L_hp (hybrid cross-modal **prototype** KL across randomly paired modalities); stage 2 freezes both the full-modality teacher and the robust model and trains a final student through a Feature Selection Module. Datasets DELIVER, MCubeS, MUSES; backbones AnySeg and CMNeXt. Student perturbation = modality dropout only. Category (a), missing-only. Verified. — [arXiv 2505.12861](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12861)
- RMMSS states the generic recipe as background: "The initial self-distillation and modality masking framework typically consists of a teacher model pretrained on full-modality data and a student model trained with randomly dropped modalities", loss L_CE + λ·L_KL; and, in related work, "In self-distillation, perturbations such as **modality masking and noisy inputs** are introduced during student training, while a full-modality teacher model is used to guide the student toward correct learning (Zhou et al. 2023; Shin et al. 2024; Hou et al. 2024)". The abstract opens with "current MMSS methods typically use self-distillation with modality dropout to improve robustness". i.e. the authors already describe teacher(full, clean)→student(perturbed) self-distillation as the **standard** practice of the field. — [arXiv 2505.12861](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12861)
- A CVPR 2026 poster titled "Towards Robust Multi-Modal Semantic Segmentation with Teacher-Student Framework and Hybrid Prototype Distillation" (RobustSeg, "teacher-student self-distillation framework with a feedback loop", +2.40% missing-modality mIoU on DeLiVER) exists; its relation to the arXiv v2 with an AAAI copyright line is not resolved (possible separate/extended version). — [CVPR 2026 poster page](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/virtual/2026/poster/39865); [arXiv 2505.12861v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.12861v2)
- **FuseAdapt — "Parameter-Efficient Multimodal Fusion for Semantic Segmentation with Missing Modalities"** (Xin Zhang, Robby T. Tan; NUS; NeurIPS 2026; arXiv 2610.21779v2 — the arXiv id in the brief is correct). Frozen DINOv2-B (DELIVER, MCubeS) / DINOv2-S (MUSES) with per-modality Q/V LoRA kept private and FFN low-rank residuals routed across modalities. Robustness objective = Predictive Modality Modeling: mask a target modality and predict its latent tokens from visible ones, supervised by an **EMA teacher (μ=0.999) that "always receives the full input"**, cosine loss, stop-gradient. Verified. — [arXiv 2509.24505](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.24505)

### Inferences
- The Scheme's **structure** (frozen teacher trained on clean full-modality data → student with perturbed input → KL on logits on top of the supervised loss) is described by RMMSS as the field's "initial self-distillation and modality masking framework" and is implemented by AnySeg and RMMSS on DELIVER/MUSES/MCubeS; FuseAdapt uses the logit-KL variant as a baseline row. A reviewer familiar with the HKUST(GZ) line (Xu Zheng et al.) will recognise it immediately.
- The Scheme's **delta** relative to these is the student input: per-sensor, sensor-specific synthetic degradation (blur/exposure, depth holes, beam drop, event down-sampling) in addition to removal. No verified MMSS paper on DELIVER/MUSES/MCubeS trains the student on such degradations; "noisy modality" is handled only at evaluation (NM protocol) and FuseAdapt lists corruption-aware targets as future work.
- RMMSS's related-work sentence ("perturbations such as modality masking and noisy inputs are introduced during student training") shows that the community already frames noisy-input students as part of the same self-distillation family, which weakens any claim that degrading (rather than dropping) the student input is conceptually new; the defensible novelty is narrower (sensor-specific operator set, 4-sensor setting, degradation ≠ missing, empirical results under NM/corner cases).

## Key Question 3 — RGB-D / RGB-T / RGB-event works that explicitly pair a clean pass and a degraded pass

### Takeaway
Yes: CRM (ICRA 2024, RGB-thermal) is an explicit clean-pass vs degraded-pass self-distillation in multi-modal segmentation, and it degrades *within* each modality (complementary patch masking), not only by dropping a modality. It is the closest verified precedent for "student sees a partially corrupted copy of both sensors, teacher signal comes from the clean pair", but it uses the same network (no frozen teacher), an L1 loss on class logits, masking only, and two sensors.

### Cited Findings
- **CRM — "Complementary Random Masking for RGB-Thermal Semantic Segmentation"** (Ukcheol Shin, Kyunghyun Lee, In So Kweon, Jean Oh; CMU / KAIST; ICRA 2024; arXiv 2303.17386). MFNet, PST900, KAIST-Pedestrian(KP); RGB-T Mask2Former with Swin backbones. Proposes "1) a complementary random masking strategy of RGB-T images and 2) self-distillation loss between clean and masked input modalities". Patches of one modality are masked (replaced by learnable tokens) and the other modality is masked with the complementary mask. Losses: L_SDC = L1(z_c(X_rgb, X_thr), z_c(X̂_rgb, X̂_thr)) and L_SDN = L1 between the clean RGB-T class logits and the logits from a single masked modality, added to modality-wise supervised losses. The motivation explicitly lists "sensor disconnection, lens occlusion, and other input quality degeneration". Same network for both passes (self-distillation; no separate frozen/EMA teacher described). Category (b). Verified. — [arXiv 2303.17386](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17386)
- CRM positions itself against prior RGB-T distillation: HeatNet distils daytime predictions to nighttime; MS-UDA and CEKD distil an RGB-T network into a thermal-only network; "this paper specifically focuses on knowledge distillation between clean and masked images for RGB-T semantic segmentation". — [arXiv 2303.17386](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17386)
- **RTFDNet — "Fusion-Decoupling for Robust RGB-T Segmentation"** (Kunyu Tan, Mingjian Liang; arXiv 2603.09149). MFNet, FMB, PST900. Three-branch network; unimodal decoders are supervised by stop-gradient targets derived from the *fused* branch (L2 on decoupled features; L1 on class-masked fused probabilities). Teacher signal = fused branch on the same clean input → unimodal branches; no synthetic degradation in training; robustness tested by dropping a modality. Not clean-vs-degraded. It characterises CRM as integrating robustness "via self-distillation with complementary masking" and notes traditional approaches "distill the multimodal knowledge of an RGB-T teacher into a single-modal student". Verified. — [arXiv 2603.09149](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09149)
- RMMSS cites SDSTrack (Hou et al., CVPR 2024, "Self-distillation symmetric adapter learning for multi-modal visual object tracking") alongside CRM as self-distillation work where "modality masking and noisy inputs" perturb the student while a full-modality teacher guides it. — [arXiv 2505.12861](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12861)
- UniMRSeg (RGB-D semantic segmentation, RGB-D/T salient object segmentation) enforces complete-vs-incomplete prediction consistency (see KQ2). — [arXiv 2509.16170](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.16170)
- UP-Fuse (camera–LiDAR) forms clean/corrupted camera pairs but uses them for uncertainty supervision, not distillation (see KQ1). — [arXiv 2602.19349](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.19349)

### Inferences
- CRM + AnySeg/RMMSS together cover almost every structural element of the Scheme: CRM supplies "clean pair vs partially degraded pair, consistency on logits"; AnySeg/RMMSS supply "separately pre-trained frozen full-modality teacher, KL, four sensors on DELIVER/MUSES". What neither supplies is sensor-specific physical degradation operators (blur/exposure, depth holes, beam drop, event down-sampling) applied independently per sensor.
- For RGB-D with noisy depth, RGB-event, or low-light RGB, this search found no verified multi-modal paper that distils a clean pass into a pass with *photometric/geometric* corruption of the sensors.

### Gaps
- SDSTrack's exact mechanism (what "noisy inputs"/masked patches are used, whether the clean branch is frozen) was not read; only RMMSS's citation is verified.
- Single-modality precedents that a professor may be recalling were not searched in this assignment (scope = multi-modal only): e.g. clean→adverse-condition distillation for camera depth (md4all, Robust-Depth), AugMix-style clean/augmented consistency, noisy-student training. These are from memory and unverified here.
- No verified RGB-D "noisy depth teacher–student" or RGB-event degradation-distillation paper was found; absence in two searches is not proof of absence.

## Key Question 4 — Frozen vision foundation models (DINOv2/DINOv3, SAM) with adapters/LoRA for multi-modal perception that include robustness training

### Takeaway
VFM-based multi-modal robustness work exists in two flavours: frozen DINOv2 + LoRA with an EMA full-input teacher for missing modalities (FuseAdapt, NeurIPS 2026, same three benchmarks as the Scheme), and SAM-based camera–LiDAR detectors where robustness comes from corruption-aware SAM pre-training (RoboFusion/RoboDistill). None of the verified ones trains a degraded multi-sensor student against a clean frozen teacher.

### Cited Findings
- FuseAdapt: frozen DINOv2-B/S shared across modalities, modality-private Q/V LoRA, routed FFN low-rank residuals; missing-modality robustness via EMA full-input teacher and latent prediction; logit-KL from the full-input EMA teacher is a weaker control (49.84 vs 52.89 mean mIoU on MCubeS MISS). DELIVER four-modality predefined-condition mIoU: FuseAdapt 67.36 mean vs Any2Seg 67.19, trailing at Night and Under-Exposure. — [arXiv 2610.21779](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21779)
- FuseAdapt lists related PEFT fusion works without robustness training claims: StitchFusion (MultiAdapters between encoder streams), MLE-SAM (modality-specific LoRA experts + routing), MemorySAM, CWSAM, concurrent M4-SAM. — [arXiv 2610.21779](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21779)
- RoboFusion: SAM / FastSAM / MobileSAM image encoder; ablation shows fine-tuning SAM beats freezing it (KITTI-C snow 57.43 vs 45.11); SAM-AD pre-training with DMAE on weather-corrupted images raises KITTI-C snow from 57.43 to 80.68. The corrupted→clean denoising objective is a *pre-training* step on images, not a teacher–student scheme on fused predictions. — [arXiv 2401.03907](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.03907)
- RoboDistill: adds KD from SAM-AD into the point-cloud branch (α=0.5, τ=4); ablation on KITTI-C snow 83.99 → 85.50 with KD. — [arXiv 2609.23541](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23541)
- UP-Fuse: frozen Swin image encoder; clean/corrupted feature divergence supervises an uncertainty MLP. — [arXiv 2602.19349](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.19349)
- "Multimodal SAM-adapter for Semantic Segmentation" (Univ. of Bologna; arXiv 2509.10408) surfaced in search; not opened. — [alphaXiv 2509.10408](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2509.10408)

### Inferences
- A frozen-DINO + LoRA multi-sensor segmentor with a teacher for robustness is already published (FuseAdapt, DINOv2); a DINOv3-based variant with a clean frozen teacher and degraded student would be distinguishable mainly by (i) degradation rather than absence, (ii) separately frozen clean teacher + logit KL rather than EMA latent prediction — and FuseAdapt reports that logit KL is the weaker of the two objectives for missing modalities, which a reviewer may raise.

### Gaps
- No DINOv3-based multi-modal segmentation paper with robustness training was found in this search (searches did not target DINOv3 specifically).
- MLE-SAM, MemorySAM, Multimodal SAM-adapter: whether any uses noise/dropout training is unverified.

## Overall conclusion — does a clean-teacher → degraded-multi-sensor-student distillation scheme already exist?

### Takeaway
Partly yes. The teacher–student skeleton exists and is considered standard in multi-modal segmentation (frozen/EMA full-modality teacher → perturbed-input student, KL/L1 on logits, on DELIVER/MUSES/MCubeS/MFNet), and clean-pass→corrupted-pass consistency with sensor-specific synthetic faults exists for camera+LiDAR driving (VG-SAF, 2026) and LiDAR (Robo3D, 2023). What was **not** found in any verified paper is the exact combination: a separately trained frozen clean teacher on ≥3 heterogeneous sensors, a student whose every sensor is independently corrupted by sensor-specific synthetic degradations (not just dropped/masked), and logit KL for semantic segmentation.

### Cited Findings
- Closest by task/benchmark: AnySeg (frozen PML-trained multimodal teacher → anymodal-dropout student; feature KL, cross-modal correspondence KL, prediction-level distillation; DELIVER, MUSES) and RMMSS/RobustSeg (pre-trained full-modality teacher; L_CE + λ·L_KL + prototype KL; DELIVER, MCubeS, MUSES). Difference from the Scheme: student perturbation is modality dropout only. — [arXiv 2411.17141](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.17141); [arXiv 2505.12861](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12861)
- Closest by "clean vs degraded copy of all sensors": CRM (RGB-T; complementary patch masking of both sensors; L1 self-distillation from clean-pair logits to masked-pair and masked-single-modality logits; same network). Difference: masking only, two sensors, no frozen teacher. — [arXiv 2303.17386](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17386)
- Closest by "sensor-specific synthetic degradation + clean reference": VG-SAF (camera+LiDAR; physically grounded fault augmentor; noisy branch pulled to stop-gradient clean branch; variance distillation). Difference: end-to-end driving, same network, variance/consistency rather than a frozen segmentation teacher. — [arXiv 2608.24366](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24366)
- The logit-KL-from-full-teacher form is treated as a generic baseline, not a contribution, in FuseAdapt (NeurIPS 2026): "Output KD matches partial-input student logits to full-context EMA teacher logits through pixelwise KL divergence". — [arXiv 2610.21779](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21779)
- RMMSS states that self-distillation with "modality masking and noisy inputs" on the student and a full-modality teacher is existing practice. — [arXiv 2505.12861](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.12861)
- In camera–LiDAR 3D detection/BEV, verified robust-fusion methods (MetaBEV, MoME, Grace-BEV, SB-BEVFusion, PFS) use modality dropout / corruption augmentation without a clean teacher. — [arXiv 2304.09801](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.09801); [arXiv 2503.19776](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19776); [arXiv 2605.30983](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30983); [arXiv 2605.11799](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11799); [arXiv 2603.05623](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05623)

### Inferences
- The professor's "I think I have seen this before" is well founded at the level of the mechanism: AnySeg / RMMSS (same benchmarks, frozen clean teacher, KL), CRM (clean vs masked self-distillation), M3L (multi-modal mean teacher → masked student) and FuseAdapt's Output-KD baseline all instantiate it.
- Distance of the closest work (AnySeg/RMMSS) to the Scheme: identical on teacher (frozen, clean, full-modality), loss family (KL on logits/predictions + supervised CE) and benchmark; different on (1) student corruption = sensor-specific degradation of present sensors vs whole-modality dropout, (2) what else is distilled (they add feature/prototype/cross-modal terms), (3) backbone. So the Scheme should be presented as an extension of the modality-dropout self-distillation family from *missing* to *degraded* sensors, with the novelty claim limited to the degradation operator design and its measured effect (e.g. under NM / DELIVER corner cases / MUSES adverse conditions), not to "distilling a clean teacher into a perturbed student" per se.
- Mandatory citations for a related-work paragraph on this component: AnySeg, RMMSS/RobustSeg, CRM, M3L, FuseAdapt (PMM and its Output-KD control), the 2503.18445 benchmark (NM protocol), and — for the degradation side — VG-SAF, UP-Fuse, Robo3D, MetaBEV/CMT/MoME (dropout/augmentation only).

### Gaps
- Coverage is not exhaustive: roughly 20 papers were read in text; several named in the brief (RoboBEV, Dong et al. CVPR 2023 benchmark, UniBEV, CMT, CAFuser, DGFusion, StitchFusion, GeminiFusion, MemorySAM, MLE-SAM, FunEntropy, GeomPrompt, MAGIC++ in full) were not opened. A paper that adds synthetic noise to the student in an AnySeg-style pipeline could exist among MLE-SAM / FPT / MAGIC++ follow-ups or in 2026 venues not indexed by the searches used.
- Radar-inclusive (RGB+LiDAR+event+radar) degradation-distillation: nothing found; only EQUISeg uses MUSES with radar, without degradation training.
- Single-modality clean→corrupted distillation literature (large, and likely part of what the professor recalls) was out of scope and not searched.
