\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented a method for adapting foundation models to multi-modal maritime semantic segmentation under varying sensor conditions.
By building on MemorySAM's memory-based cross-modal framework, we introduced three complementary design choices: (1) soft mixture-of-experts gating for LoRA adaptation, which ensures full expert utilization when the number of experts is small; (2) a cross-modal fusion head that replaces independent sigmoid-based scoring with joint softmax-normalized comparison, applied through dual paths---max-normalized for memory modulation and raw softmax for output fusion; and (3) aggressive RGB corruption augmentations for day-to-night generalization.
Our experiments on the MULTIAQUA benchmark validate each design choice and demonstrate the effectiveness of relative cross-modal scoring for adaptive modality weighting in degraded sensing conditions.
